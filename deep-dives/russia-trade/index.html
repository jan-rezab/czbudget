READING THE EVIDENCE

A strong signal is not a traced shipment.

01

One reporting side

All three legs use export declarations. Russian imports are not added to mirror exports. Values are current USD, exports generally FOB; prices and exchange rates are not held constant.

02

Same code, different shipments

The selection holds one HS6 code: processors and controllers, portable computers, machining centres or ball bearings. HS 2017 and HS 2022 are retained with observations; the selected codes are unchanged in the WCO’s HS 2022 impact table. A code does not establish identical models, prices, origin or end use.

03

Coverage is not the whole world

One selected exporter and one selected intermediary. This is not a total for world trade or all of Russia’s neighbours. Missing declarations do not mean zero, and recent months can be incomplete.

04

Co-movement is not causation

Imports can serve domestic demand, production or inventories. Exports to Russia can have a different origin and timing. Identifying a specific re-export or sanctions violation requires additional evidence. The three legs are never summed.
