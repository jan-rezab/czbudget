05 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Test the hypothesis.
Don’t assume the conclusion.

What we aggregate

Amounts are calculated sums of available original HS6 records in current USD. Each latest revised observation enters once. World partner rows are never added to bilateral rows. These are observed merchandise subtotals, not the official TOTAL series or inflation-adjusted volumes.

Two reporting perspectives

The map uses the hubs’ import declarations and their exports to Russia. The final table separately uses suppliers’ export declarations. Imports are generally CIF, exports FOB. Mirror values can differ because of valuation, timing, origin or errors; they are never added together.

A verified coverage limitation

Audit: 26 September 2026. Kazakhstan’s available detail for 2020–2022 is below national totals. For 2021, the warehouse HS6 import subtotal is USD 27,751,611,648.923; the ministry reported USD 41.2 billion for all merchandise imports. We do not splice or estimate the difference. The comparison uses 2019; warehouse changes are not estimates of the entire market.

Co-movement is not the same shipment

Growth can reflect domestic demand, local production, prices, inventories or re-export. HS2 is a broad category; HS revisions can change its composition. The four selectable HS6 codes are unchanged between HS 2017 and 2022. These tables do not prove sanctions violations or that the entire direct-trade decline moved to these hubs.

Coverage and source provenance

Research framework

EBRD’s research compares direct and intermediated trade at HS6 level and distinguishes sanctioned products. Our tables are descriptive exploration: without a product-level sanctions list, control group and transaction records, they do not provide a causal estimate.
