01 / THE COUNTRY BOUNDARY

Connected to the world.

Goods and services are only the beginning. Wages, investment income and transfers also cross the border. These are the four parts of the current account.

CZK billion per year

Capital has two directions.

Foreign investors acquire Czech assets; Czech residents acquire assets abroad. These financial-account entries measure net transactions in each instrument, not gross cash payments.

02 / DOMESTIC CIRCULATION

One transaction. Two sides.

A wage is an employer’s expense and a household’s income. Choose a transaction to see who records the payment and who records the receipt, including foreign counterparties.

Production is a different measure.

Businesses buy inputs from other businesses. GDP removes that repeated production value; it does not add up every payment.

03 / MONEY, FINANCE & PAYMENTS

What moves. What is held.

Money stocks, financing transactions and payment turnover answer different questions. Their totals overlap and must remain separate.

The payment system

Transfers and cards include business payments. Settlement turnover overlaps with client payments; cash withdrawals do not measure cash purchases.

04 / OPEN THE ACCOUNTS

Every number has a boundary.

The full inventory includes missing observations. Open any measure for its exact source value, unit, period and definition.

An atlas, with the boundaries intact.

National accounts and the balance of payments use accrual accounting. Financial flows are generally net; payment statistics are gross; stocks refer to a date. Missing values stay missing. No inferred bilateral payments, no synthetic balancing flow, and no sum across these layers.
