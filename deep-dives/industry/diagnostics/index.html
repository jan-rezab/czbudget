Methodology

What the data say — and where they stop.

Same selection, same definition

Only identical series dimensions are compared. Units, industry, business size, institutional sector and adjustment remain separate. NACE Rev. 2 and 2.1 and historical/new SBS are not automatically spliced.

Observations, not causal estimates

Capacity and order books come from DG ECFIN surveys. FIGARO models industry relationships. PRODCOM describes products, SBS enterprises by their main activity. Co-movement alone does not establish causation.

Missing does not mean zero

Confidential and missing values stay empty; lines do not bridge them. Flags and original cells appear in the table and CSV. PRODCOM quantity units are specific to each product and year.

History from 2010

The requested period starts in 2010. Actual coverage depends on the source. The archive is the currently available revised history, not a reconstruction of information available in earlier years.
