01 / Primary mark

A percent sign built from the palette.

Every headline number on the portal is a share of GDP, so the unit becomes the mark. The green and red squares survive from the old four-square grid; the paper slash between them turns the grid into a percent sign that still reads at favicon size.

04 / Chart treatment

The ranking bar, with country identity added.

Same grammar as the homepage hero: ink panel, monospace furniture, one green series and a single red highlight. What is new is the flag and the ISO tag, which let a reader find their country without reading every label. Values are live from lib/data/sovereign-benchmark.v1.json.
