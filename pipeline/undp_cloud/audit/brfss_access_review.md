# BRFSS source-access review, 27 September 2026

The original CDC annual microdata sources remain the intended intake. The parent observed HTTP 403 for the current four CDC descriptors in its cloud source run; this review did not rerun that job, download archives, or bypass the response. The historical intake plan remains `brfss_historical_fetch_plan.json` (1991–2025). Listing a public link is not evidence of a successful cloud load.

The official [2024 annual documentation](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/annual_data/annual_2024.html) remains readable through the web tool and offers ASCII and SAS Transport archives. It reports **457,670 records**, **345 variables** in the XPT file, and an August 2025 release. These are respondent records in the combined landline/cell-phone public-use file, covering 49 states, DC, Guam, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands; Tennessee is absent. The page explicitly warns that questions were removed from the current dataset and that 2024 is not directly comparable with pre-2011 weighting/sampling. Both original revision provenance and item definitions must therefore be retained.

The official [2025 annual documentation](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/annual_data/annual_2025.html), reviewed 9 September 2026, reports **356,158 records**, **284 variables** in the XPT file, and an August 2026 release. The combined survey covers 47 states, DC, Guam and Puerto Rico; California, Mississippi, Nevada and the US Virgin Islands are absent. This is a newer annual release, not a real-time respondent feed. The original annual microdata and corresponding year-specific codebooks/module weights remain necessary for HDR Figures S3.1.1 and S3.1.2.

No currently accessible official full-microdata mirror was verified. The official [CDC FTP `/pub/data/` directory](https://ftp.cdc.gov/pub/data/) presently lists DASH, GSHS, MINING and YRBS, with no BRFSS entry. Historical references to an FTP BRFSS directory do not establish present availability. The annual page's ASCII archive is an official alternative format; its cloud accessibility has not been tested, and it requires the exact SAS layout, missing-value definitions and year-specific weighting. It is not admitted as a successful replacement.

CDC provides [prevalence/analysis tools](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/data_tools.htm) and the official [2011-present prevalence catalogue](https://data.cdc.gov/Behavioral-Risk-Factors/Behavioral-Risk-Factor-Surveillance-System-BRFSS-P/dttw-5yxu). These are aggregate indicators, not interchangeable with the precise individual age/sex, life-satisfaction and exactly-30-days mental-health definitions used in the HDR figures. The small Socrata metadata endpoint `https://data.cdc.gov/api/views/dttw-5yxu.json` was not accessible through the web tool; a bounded local metadata request could not resolve the host in the network sandbox. No claim of verified current API schema or exact indicator coverage is made.

Third-party archived copies are not official mirrors and were not substituted. No historical archive was downloaded to the Mac. Full HDR trend reproduction remains unavailable until exact original survey years, item definitions, optional-module jurisdiction coverage, source revision and survey weights are loaded and validated. The 2011 raking/cell-phone sampling break must remain visible.
