## Reconstruction acceptance

Each statistical panel needs source release/hash, all source years and native units, exact geography/category/denominator, estimator/filters, published-value benchmark and documented missing codes. Computed quantities are labelled calculations. Fixed experiments do not receive invented annual history. Source-specific licence/access conditions travel with the records. Preserve all native historical rows in versioned macro exports, with original source snapshots separate from newer revisions.

The machine-readable sibling provides per-object source contracts, immutable receipt references and hashes, method-specific requirements, official errata and the full six-chapter mapping. Conceptual and restricted gaps remain part of the story rather than becoming proxy data.
