# EU capital budgets (2026 source set)

This source set covers the 27 European Union capitals plus London and powers the published `eu-capitals.html` comparison.

## Files

- `eu_capital_budget_sources_2026.json` is the reviewed source registry and records the authoritative local-currency figures, reporting scope, period, status, and caveats.
- `eu_capital_budgets_2026.json` is generated by `scripts/prepare_eu_capital_budgets.py`; it adds the normalized EUR value and source archive metadata.
- `eu_capital_city_metrics.json` is the long-form population and tourism benchmark layer generated by `scripts/prepare_eu_capital_benchmarks.py`.
- `source_cache/eu_capital_budgets_2026/` contains downloaded official publications or official web pages with SHA-256 checksums in the generated dataset. A text proxy is used when an official server rejects archival clients; two anti-bot-protected pages are represented by explicit reviewed source snapshots.
- `website/data/eu-capital-budgets.v1.json` is the sole public mirror used by the cities UI.

## Currency convention

The official local-currency amount is authoritative. EUR values use one archived and machine-validated ECB reference-rate snapshot dated 2026-08-20 and the formula `local_amount / local_currency_units_per_eur`. A future currency switch should read `budget.local_amount`, `budget.local_currency`, and `budget.eur_amount` rather than reconverting in the browser.

## Comparability

The broadest expenditure total available in each official publication is retained, but municipal systems are not uniform. Berlin and Vienna are city-states; Brussels is the core municipality; London is the GLA group and excludes boroughs; Dublin's official figure is an operating budget; some sources include financing or debt repayment. Use `measure`, `scope`, `components`, and `notes` in the UI.

Valletta is a documented exception: the council's official 2026 PDF contains only a cover and contents page. The dataset uses its latest complete official budget (2025) and flags the period and status.

Population comes from the latest available Eurostat City Statistics observation. Tourism uses 2024 total accommodation nights, non-resident nights where available, non-resident share, and nights per resident. Every observation retains its year, geography label, comparability group, and quality flags. Dublin and London use official local tourism fallbacks because they are absent from Eurostat's selected-city tourism table.

## BigQuery model

`gcp/bigquery/schema.sql` defines a shared municipal model rather than a capital-only silo:

- `public_entities` is the stable entity dimension. Czech municipalities resolve through `national_entity_code_type = 'CZ_ICO'` and `national_entity_code`; Prague uses IČO `00064581` and reuses `public_entity_id = 'prague-cz'`.
- `public_entity_budget_headlines` stores the annual headline totals used by the capitals page.
- `municipal_budget_line_facts` is reserved for FIN 2-12M detail at paragraph × economic item × budget stage grain. `is_consolidation_item = TRUE` means the row is an internal-transfer/consolidation item to exclude from consolidated totals.
- `public_entity_balance_sheet_facts` and `public_entity_cash_facts` keep accounting and cash observations separate from budget execution.
- `public_entity_metric_observations` stores population and tourism benchmarks in long form.

BigQuery-ready JSONL seeds are generated in `gcp/seed/` by `gcp/prepare_city_seed_data.py`.

## Rebuild

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare_eu_capital_budgets.py --refresh
python3 scripts/prepare_eu_capital_benchmarks.py --refresh
python3 gcp/prepare_city_seed_data.py
```

Omit `--refresh` to reuse archived sources. The command exits non-zero if any official source cannot be archived, while still writing a dataset that records every failed source explicitly.
